Atlas · Chad O&G market (operator-owned assets)

Mapping of Chad O&G infrastructure · 3 canon pillars.

Cartography structured by 3 canon pillars: Upstream (5 basins) · Midstream (1,070 km pipeline · Komé storage · FSO Kribi) · Downstream (raffinerie refinery · 200 service stations · 50 IRVE 2030). Chad installed electric capacity: 125 MW.

⚠ Important note · EnerTchad positioning

EnerTchad does not own any oil assets. Our company, founded 2026, starts as oilfield services provider (OFS) to existing operators in Chad: opérateur amont 1, opérateur amont 2, opérateur national, opérateurs pipeline, raffinerie. Our progressive ambition is to migrate toward full E&P activity by 2030+ via targeted exploration licenses.
