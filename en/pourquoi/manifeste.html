CEO Manifesto · April 2026

For Chad's energy sovereignty, operational and proud.

By Bignéro Moïalbéi Le Madang · Chief Executive Officer · ex-ExxonMobil Doba project

For fifteen years, I worked for international O&G majors. I contributed to the Doba project at ExxonMobil. I saw, from inside, how technical petroleum services operate at global scale.

I also witnessed every month airplanes landing in N'Djamena with expatriate technical teams for missions Chadian engineers — bilingual, trained, motivated — could perfectly execute. Each rotation: $25-40 K fly-in/fly-out fees, hourly coefficients 2.8 to 3.5× higher, and value-add leaving the country.

This anomaly is not the result of a deliberate decision by operators. It results from an absence: no local services player has, until today, proven its ability to deliver to majors' technical standards while remaining under OHADA governance with a national team.

Three pillars structure our approach.

Pillar 01

Sovereignty

OHADA SA/CA · 10 M FCFA capital · N'Djamena HQ · 75% nationals at 18 months. No skill expatriation.

Pillar 02

Technical Excellence

API · ISO · IEC standards. Halliburton/SLB/Yokogawa/Claroty partnerships (negotiating). ISO 9001/14001/45001 certifications by Q3 2027.

Pillar 03

Transparency

EITI 2013 reporting from Q1 2027. Public annual financial report. ISO 37001 anti-corruption. External whistleblowing. Transparency is our license to operate.

Our differentiating innovation.

Where majors import their EOR consumables (HPAM polymers, alkalis, surfactants), we develop 100% local sourcing: Local EOR Program "Chadian Resources" — Borkou Natron, Arabic Gum (Chad 2nd world producer), Cotton Cellulose (250 000 ha · 2.5 M workers), Neem & Karité (biosurfactants), Lake Chad Spirulina (microbial MEOR). 3-phase roadmap · 100% local by 2032. First IRVE operator in CEMAC zone with 50 kW CCS2/CHAdeMO charging stations from Q3 2027.

« We do not sell oil. We are building Chad's energy sovereignty, basin after basin, station after station, job after job. » Bignéro Moïalbéi Le Madang · CEO EnerTchad Groupe SA/CA · ex-ExxonMobil
