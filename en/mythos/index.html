IThe Land

Chad has carried its black gold since 1973.

The Doba basin awakens in 2003 under major nord-américaine-major O&G internationale's drilling. One billion barrels of proven reserves sleep beneath the Logone sands. For two decades, foreign majors extract, refine, export. Chadian crude travels 1,070 km of pipeline to Kribi in Cameroon. It feeds global markets. It contributes 35% of state revenues. It draws the financial silhouette of the country.

But one truth remains, etched in Cretaceous sandstone: Chadian oil has always been extracted by others. Technical services — drilling, completion, EOR, pipeline integrity, refining, distribution — have been steered from Houston, Beijing, Paris, Calgary. Trained Chadian engineers migrated to global majors. Know-how stayed abroad.

Oil leaves our soil. The services that bring it out — they don't leave with it. — Observation 2024 · Chadian diaspora, OFS sector
IIThe Departure

In 2024, major O&G internationale withdraws.

The event is not a press release. It is a tilt. Esso Exploration & Production Chad transfers its assets to Tchad Petroleum Compagnie (TPC), the new national operator. CNPCIC, Perenco, SHT, COTCO, SRN remain. But the chain of technical providers redraws itself overnight. The market demands local OFS operators capable of absorbing contracts that external majors no longer sign.

At the same moment, in N'Djamena, Bignéro Moïalbéi Le Madang completes twelve years inside an international major in Chad and Cameroon. He has seen from within how an integrated services operation works — OIMS standards, major O&G internationale-grade HSE procedures, IEC 62443 pipelines. He knows what the Chadian market lacks: a Chadian company capable of serving operators technically with majors-grade standards.

EnerTchad SA is born in 2026, in this transition window. Not earlier — the majors would have had to be displaced. Not later — TPC and CNPCIC would already have found providers from elsewhere. Now.

IIIThe Vocation

We are oil-services specialists, not explorers.

The opening choice is sharp. EnerTchad SA will own no E&P assets. No exploration block. No oil licence. No mature-field acquisition. The company is 100% Oil Field Services (OFS) — pure technical vocation in service of market operators.

This choice is not retreat. It is discipline. The capital required for E&P would be tens of millions USD just for land and seismic. Returns would stretch over fifteen years. The 2026 Seed/Pre-A tour targets USD 8-12 M — a sum sized for the OFS pivot, where each dollar finances equipment, contracts, trained staff, and OAPI patents directly.

The OFS pivot also enables narrative honesty: we are not selling a "African oil Eldorado" greenwash. We are selling a catalogue of 40+ technical services structured by codes (S/D/C/U/P/T/E/H), 4 canonical poles, 3 transversal axes, and 6 OAPI-registered trademarks™. Everything is measurable, contractible, OHADA-compliant.

IVThe Return

The Chadian diaspora comes home.

We count several hundred Chadian engineers, technicians, and managers trained at top OFS international, major O&G internationale, major européenne, top OFS international, top OFS international — spread across Houston, Calgary, Aberdeen, Paris, Doha, Luanda. This human capital in exile is the most valuable strategic resource for EnerTchad SA.

Our Local Content Development commitment is radical: 75 to 90% Chadian diaspora talents by 2030. Not a cosmetic ESG slogan — an auditable numerical target. EnerAcademy™ — our OAPI brand for training — targets 200 FTEs trained per year from 2030, to IADC RIG PASS, IWCF, and IOGP Life-Saving Rules standards.

When a diaspora engineer comes back to operate in Doba, it is not patriotism. It is economics: he becomes one of the best-paid technicians in the Sahel for standards he already masters. — LCD logic · EnerAcademy 2030 vision
VThe Resources

Five Chadian resources become OAPI patents.

EnerTchad SA's Local EOR Programme is our signature R&D. We identify five Chadian natural or agricultural resources — cotton, neem, gum arabic, Lake Chad spirulina, indigenous microbial strains — and transform them into patented formulations for Enhanced Oil Recovery.

Each of these patents, filed with OAPI (African Intellectual Property Organisation · 17 member states), is valid for 50 years renewable. Early laboratory tests show gains of +6 to +17% OOIP on Doba mature fields — performance comparable to imported chemical additives, at a domestic cost 30-40% lower.

The bet is simple: if Chadian black gold is extracted with Chadian additives, then a significant share of the value chain no longer leaves the country. Chad ceases to be a producer of raw commodity. It becomes a territory of Africa-first oil innovation.

VIThe Threshold

2026 is our threshold. 2050 is our cap.

The 2026 Seed/Pre-A tour closes on 30 September 2026. Eight to twelve million USD will be raised under OHADA structure. The calendar is public: 47% closed at May 2026 (USD 4.7 M soft committed), 12 NDAs signed, 3 operator MoUs signed. Series A USD 15-25 M projected for 2027. Series B USD 50-80 M for 2028. Series C for 2030.

Beyond the financial trajectory, we commit to a climate destiny. EnerTchad SA will reach Net Zero scope 1+2+3 in 2050, aligned with the Paris Agreement and the IEA NZE scenario. Trajectory in 5 phases: Foundation (2026-27) → Reduction (2027-30) → Transition (2030-35) → Acceleration (2035-45) → Net Zero (2045-50). Each phase has its public milestones, auditable KPIs, target certifications (ISO 14001, 45001, EITI, MSCI ESG).

The radical-sustainability bet is also an economic calculation: by 2030, international DFIs will refuse to finance any oil-services company without a credible Net Zero trajectory. EnerTchad SA makes the commitment now, because it is easier now than after five years of operations without a structured ESG framework.

VIIThe Horizon

An oil-services firm becomes an African energy platform.

By 2030, EnerTchad SA will have deployed 50 EV charging stations across the CEMAC zone — first multi-energy operator in Central Africa. Mobile Station™ S/M/L will offer petrol, diesel, electricity (CCS2/CHAdeMO 50 kW), and soon hydrogen. NRJ+™ will have captured 8 to 12% of Chad's premium fuel market. EnerFert™ will have industrialised specialty fertilisers from Djermaya petrochemicals.

By 2050, the trajectory is more radical still. We will have become a platform of decarbonised technical services operating across multiple OHADA states. Our OAPI patents will cover the next generations of microbial EOR. EnerAcademy™ will have trained several thousand pan-African technicians. The company may have absorbed decarbonised energy assets (CCUS, green hydrogen, Lake Chad biodiversity).

But the story is simpler than the strategy. We were born from Chadian soil. We remain faithful to Chadian soil. We will transform the wealth extracted from this soil into durable competencies that will never leave it again.

Chad will not be the next African oil Eldorado. Chad will be the first African country to have built an OFS that exceeds external majors — on the ground, at majors-grade standards, while remaining at home. — Cap 2030 vision · CEO Bignéro Moïalbéi Le Madang
