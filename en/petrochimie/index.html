🟣 Petrochemicals Division · OFS

Petrochemicals Services · Chad associated gas valorization
fertilizers · methanol · polymers.

EnerTchad delivers technical services to the petrochemical industry of the Chad market. We do not own any production unit. Our services cover existing units optimization (HSE process safety · NDT inspection · turnaround maintenance) and pre-FEED for new units envisioned by operators: fertilizers (urea/ammonia) · methanol · basic polymers · specialty chemicals.
