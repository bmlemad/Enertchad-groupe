03 · 6 quantified HSE KPIs · 2030 targets

Verifiable commitments · quarterly disclosure.

KPI 01

LTIF < 0.5

Lost Time Injury Frequency (per million man-hours). OFS sector median ≈ 0.7. EnerTchad target 0.5 by end-2027, 0.3 by 2030.

KPI 02

TRIR < 1.0

Total Recordable Injury Rate. Target < 1.0 end-2027. Monthly reporting · ISO 45001 quarterly audit · annual ITIE disclosure.

KPI 03

Fatalities · absolute zero

CEO commitment: zero fatalities 2026-2030. ICAM root-cause investigation for every Class ≥3 incident. Stop-work authority at every level.

KPI 04

CO₂ emissions

12 kg CO₂/barrel-equivalent (vs ~18 kg African sector). Scope 1+2 measured per GRI Standards. Net-Zero scope 1+2 target by 2045.

KPI 05

HSE training

50 FTE trained/yr Phase 1 (EnerAcademy™ HSE) · target 200/yr Phase 2. 100% staff IADC RIG PASS-equivalent certified.

KPI 06

Anti-corruption audit

100% annual ISO 37001 audit · code of conduct signed by all employees · WhistleB hotline · ITIE Chad disclosure.
