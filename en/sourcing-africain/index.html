02 · 4 priority sources · African refineries

Four refineries · four logistics corridors.

SOURCE 01 · DANGOTE NIGERIA

Dangote Refinery · Lekki, Lagos

650,000 bpd · largest private refinery in Africa · COD 2024. Euro V specifications · low-sulfur diesel · gasoline · jet A1 · LPG · paraffins · bitumens. Massive export capacity to CEMAC. Logistics corridor: tanker trucks Lagos → Maiduguri → N'Djamena (≈ 1,800 km · 7-10 days). Tariff: competitive vs European imports (12-18% savings on freight + duties). Dangote Refining accepts Naira · USD · LC payments.

650 kbpdEuro VPhase 1 Pilot
SOURCE 02 · NIGER · SORAZ

SORAZ Zinder · Border Niger

Zinder refinery (Société de Raffinage de Zinder · CNPC 60% / Niger State 40%) · ~20 kbpd · COD 2011. Configuration similar to Djermaya. Critical geographic advantage: direct border with eastern Chad (Mao · Lake Chad). Short logistics corridor: Zinder → Mao → N'Djamena (≈ 900 km · 4-6 days). Particularly relevant for eastern Chad basin + Lake. Political synergy: ALGE-NIGE-CHAD · CEMAC + UEMOA cross-border.

~20 kbpdDirect borderCEMAC + UEMOA
SOURCE 03 · ANGOLA · SONANGOL

Luanda & Lobito Refineries · Sonangol

Sonangol operates two complexes: Luanda (~65 kbpd) + Lobito (~200 kbpd, under construction). Major African producer post-OPEC. Specialties: Euro standard fuels · road bitumens · paraffins. Maritime logistics corridor: Luanda port → Pointe-Noire (RoC) → Douala (Cameroon) → reverse COTCO pipeline corridor · or Bangui (CAR) → N'Djamena. Longer but massive volumes. Payments: Kwanza · USD · EUR.

SOURCE 04 · ALGERIA · SONATRACH

Sonatrach Refineries · Skikda · Arzew · Algiers

Total Sonatrach > 600 kbpd · 5 refineries (Skikda · Arzew · Algiers · Hassi Messaoud · Adrar). Major OPEC producer. Specialties: high-quality fuels · low-sulfur diesel · special bitumens for Saharan climate · massive LPG. Trans-Saharan logistics corridor: Hassi Messaoud → Tamanrasset → Niamey → N'Djamena (≈ 3,000 km · 12-18 days · seasonal). Substantial volumes compatible with road bitumens for Chadian BTP. Alternative route: maritime Skikda → Lagos → Dangote corridor.

03 · Target sourcing mix & AfCFTA framework

Diversified sourcing mix · supply resilience.

EnerTchad does not depend on any single source. Phase 2 (2028+) target sourcing mix: 30% Dangote (Nigeria) · 25% SORAZ Niger · 20% Sonangol Angola · 15% Sonatrach Algeria · 10% Djermaya local. This mix ensures geopolitical resilience (one source disruption ≠ national shortage), pricing optimization, and AfCFTA (African Continental Free Trade Area) compliance — Chad ratified 2021. Estimated savings vs extra-African sourcing: 15-25% on the complete mix.
