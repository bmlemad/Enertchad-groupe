The anomaly

Chad extracts, transports, refines. Yet depends on expats for every technical service.

Since 2003, Chad has produced up to 130 kbpd. 1,067 km export pipeline. One refinery. 5 active basins. And yet, for every advanced technical operation — reservoir characterization, polymer EOR, ILI pipeline, OT cybersecurity, lubricants blending — an expatriate must be flown in.

This anomaly creates 20-40% logistics overhead vs. equivalent local service (Wood Mackenzie 2023, Rystad Energy 2024). It deprives the country of skilled technical jobs and tax revenues. And it slows down the Local Content obligations of production-sharing contracts.

EnerTchad exists to close this anomaly. An OHADA SA/CA provider, headquartered in N'Djamena, with Chadian teams trained at the majors, contracted technical partnerships internationally.

6 Business Units

Upstream, midstream, downstream — plus digital, green and governance.

Each business unit aggregates a set of specific job families. The upstream/midstream/downstream split follows the classic O&G value chain. The 3 cross-functional units (GreenTech · EnerTech · Governance) reflect contemporary stakes.

BU 01 · Upstream

Upstream — exploration, drilling, production, EOR

Geosciences, completion, reservoir engineering, EOR polymers/CO₂/thermal, stimulation. Main targets: opérateur amont 1 (Bongor), opérateur amont 2 (Doba).

BU 02 · Midstream

Midstream — pipeline, storage, custody transfer

ILI smart pigging, pipeline SCADA, ROW drone surveillance, fiscal metering, FSO Kribi, depots. Target: opérateurs pipeline (1,067 km).

BU 03 · Downstream

Downstream — refining, distribution, lubricants, LPG

Refinery process, lubricants blending 15W-40 / 5W-30, bitumen, LPG, 200 service stations, Mobile Station™. Target: raffinerie (unité de raffinage du marché).

BU 04 · GreenTech

Energy & GreenTech — solar, hybrid, hydrogen

Industrial solar isolated sites, gas-to-power + BESS hybridization, anti-flaring, REDD+ carbon credits, community mini-grids, EV charging. Cap 125 MW by 2030.

BU 05 · EnerTech

Technology & cybersecurity — IoT, AI, OT, drones

Digital twins, industrial IoT, ML production, predictive maintenance, pipeline drone inspection, SCADA/DCS, OT cyber IEC 62443, AI physical security, AR/VR training.

BU 06 · Governance

ESG · EITI · OHADA · Local Content

EITI 2013 reporting, technical due diligence for opérateur national, PSC contractual oversight, ISO 37001 anti-corruption, EnerAcademy training for Chadian executives.
