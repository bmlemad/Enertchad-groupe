Local EOR Program · 5 Chadian Resources

World first innovation: leveraging 5 Chadian natural resources as sustainable alternatives to imported synthetic EOR products.

Borkou Natron

Alkaline ASP

Crystal harvest from Borkou salt lakes. Alkaline ASP injection. 6-12% additional OOIP recovery.

Arabic Gum

Natural polymer

Acacia (Chad #2 world producer). Natural polymer EOR alternative to HPAM. 8-15% additional OOIP.

Cotton Cellulose

Secondary polymer

250,000 ha cotton cultivated. Microfibers as secondary EOR polymer. 3-7% additional OOIP.

Neem & Karité

Biosurfactant

Sahel plants. Natural biosurfactants reducing IFT 1000-fold. EOR alternative to synthetic surfactants.

Lake Chad Spirulina

MEOR microbial

Cyanobacteria. In-situ microbial EOR. 5-10% additional OOIP. World-lowest cost extraction.
